• Area & Volume: Trapezium = ½(a+b)h  |  Prism/Cylinder = Base Area × h  |  Cone/Pyramid = ⅓ Base Area × h
• Geometry: Polygon Angle Sum = (n − 2) × 180°  |  Pythagoras: a² + b² = c²  |  SOHCAHTOA
• Statistics & Probability: IQR = Q₃ − Q₁  |  NCEA ICI = Median ± 1.5 × IQR / √n  |  P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
